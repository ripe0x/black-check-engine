# Phase 3 economic model results

Generated by `python3 sim/model.py` (60 seeded runs per scenario, 730 days). Medians unless stated. A model, not evidence about real behavior; parameters marked in sim/model.py `base_params`.

Base: 20 Originals at 0.25 ETH, 37 Shapes (0.01-0.1 ETH backing), tier schedule reset to market monthly with a 7-day timelock, decay 0.2% per survived draw to 60% of schedule, composition at 150 survived draws (300 for Black), coin fee share 1% of volume buying 80s (max 3 per week), gas 2 gwei.

## Exact FWA economics (no randomness)

For a board with notionals p_i and market values v_i: fee F = 1.05 N / S with S = sum(1/p); player expected value per draw = sum(v_i / p_i) / S; owner keeps 1.025 N / S. If every notional equals its value, the owner's expected margin is 2.5% of N/S per draw and players get 1/1.05 of the fee back in value (less VRF). A Check listed at a share a of its value adds (1/a - 1) to sum(v_i/p_i): draws turn positive expected value for players, so arbitrage, once
sum over Checks of (1/a_k - 1) > 0.05 N + VRF/(N/S).

| Board | Floor a at which every Check at floor makes draws break even for players (VRF ignored) |
| --- | --- |
| 20 Checks among 57 listings | 0.875 |
| 40 Checks among 77 listings | 0.912 |
| 20 Checks among 30 listings | 0.930 |
| 100 Checks among 137 listings | 0.936 |

Below that floor, the protocol pays a subsidy on every Check won and arbitrage players will draw until Checks leave. Above it, decay only shifts odds between prizes.

## Simulated scenarios

Game P&L = owner proceeds - Shape replacement - market value of Checks won - keeper gas (Checks won are inventory paid for by coin fees, so this is negative by design). Operating P&L counts Checks won at their notional instead: what draws earn or lose before the prize subsidy. Subsidy = market value of Checks won - their notional. Coin fees fund purchases separately; unspent = budget left by the purchase-depth limit. P(single) = share of runs that composed at least one single Check. No run formed a Black Check unless the max tier column says Black.
