# Buyback cadence (sim/burner.py, D64)

Generated by `python3 sim/burner.py`. Launch cadence: 0.05 ETH + 0.005 ETH per draw since the last slice + 1% of the bucket, at most 1 ETH; spacing 60 minutes x 20 / (20 + draws), at least 10 minutes; released milestone ETH 1 ETH every 10 minutes, the first at once; impact at most 3%.

## Steady state: does the cadence keep up with the inflow? (30 days)

Inflow: ETH reaching the burner's ongoing bucket a day (the ongoing quarter of the game's surplus share, 0.00026 ETH a draw, plus any fee overflow). The model runs every slice as soon as it may; a keeper is reimbursed at most 5% of a slice, so in practice the tiny idle slices wait until draws or the backlog make a slice worth a keeper's gas (or a player's DrawRouter draw runs it).

## A release: how long until it is bought, and what the impact limit lets through

| release | slices of 1 ETH every 10 minutes | time | largest slice under 3% at FDV 50 / 100 / 250 ETH |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0.5 ETH | 1 | 0 min after the first | 0.74 / 1.49 / 3.72 ETH |
| 3 ETH | 3 | 20 min after the first | 0.74 / 1.49 / 3.72 ETH |
| 9 ETH | 9 | 80 min after the first | 0.74 / 1.49 / 3.72 ETH |
| 30 ETH | 30 | 290 min after the first | 0.74 / 1.49 / 3.72 ETH |

A slice larger than the impact limit allows is filled only up to the limit (the swap's price limit); the rest stays in the bucket for the next slice. At an opening FDV of 50 ETH a 1 ETH slice is cut to about 0.74 ETH; from about 67 ETH of depth a full 1 ETH slice fits.
